Prepare a meeting brief for this project.

Read _project.md, wiki/index.md, the wiki/questions/ pages, the open wiki/feedback/ entries,
and the most recent wiki/log.md entries. Then produce a brief with three parts:
1. What changed since `last_ingested` in _project.md (new findings, new questions, new sources).
2. Open questions that need a decision this meeting. For each, state the current hypothesis and
   what evidence or decision would resolve it.
3. Status of prior action items. Gather open `- [ ]` tasks from their sources: each
   wiki/feedback/ page's "Resulting actions" checklist plus the _project.md Tasks section
   (action items are single-sourced there, never mirrored). Order by the 🔴/🟡/🟢 priority
   marker (unmarked = normal) and flag anything with a `[due:: ...]` that is overdue.

Write it as slide-ready bullets in my voice (see _schema/voice.md). Keep Korean terms where the
wiki uses them, with a short gloss. Save to drafts/ as `YYYY-MM-DD-meeting-prep.md`. I paste it
into the Google Slides deck. Do not edit the wiki.
