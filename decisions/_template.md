# Decision Ledger Template

Copy one entry block per decision into the current month's file (`decisions/YYYY-MM.md`). Keep every field on one line, the reasoning field is the one that matters, and write in whichever language the decision happened in. Rationale and design: [[decision-twin-plan]].

```
## YYYY-MM-DD: one-line name of the decision

- **Domain:** research | tooling | life
- **Decision:** what was being decided, one line
- **Options:** the options actually considered
- **Choice:** the option taken
- **Reasoning:** why it won, and what would have flipped it
- **Inputs:** what information or advice was used
- **Confidence at the time:** low | medium | high
- **Outcome (fill later):** what happened, and would I decide the same again
```
